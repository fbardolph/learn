Daily closing prices, 3-day median to strip single-print spikes in thin markets, sampled every 2 days. D and R lines come from separate “Yes” contracts, so in two-way races they’re near mirror images (they can sum to 99–102% because of spread); they diverge where an independent is in play. In Nebraska, Montana, Idaho and South Dakota the “Dem odds” line uses whichever non-Republican contract (Democratic or independent) was priced higher that day; the Nebraska and Montana independent contracts opened in late March. Before primaries, some markets traded as a generic party contract and were later renamed for the nominee. Source: Polymarket Gamma + CLOB price-history APIs, history through Nov 2 is saved in the page; everything after that loads live from Polymarket each time you open it.
